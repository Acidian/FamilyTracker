# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Two parents, one on Android and one on iPhone, coordinating care for three children who do not have phones. The parent is often tired, interrupted, or managing several responsibilities at once.

## Product Purpose
A shared family organiser, starting with medicine courses, confirmed dose records, and the next thing the family needs to do. Reduce the mental load of remembering school activities, play dates, and care schedules. Children are profiles managed by the parents, not independent accounts.

## Brand Personality
Calm, reassuring, clear. The user explicitly chose the Nightlight visual concept and requested implementation on 22 September 2026.

## Anti-references
The unselected bright blue/yellow Orbit concept and warm paper Hearth concept. Avoid child-game aesthetics, clinical dashboards, decorative metrics, and dense administration screens.

## Design Principles
- Make the next action obvious and quick.
- Distinguish a planned dose from a recorded dose.
- Confirm medicine details; never infer medical instructions.
- Show who recorded an action and when.
- Be explicit about device-only data versus shared data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large touch targets, visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, reduced motion, and readable contrast. These are implementation defaults, not a claim of an accessibility certification.
